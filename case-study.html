Case study template

Engineering, documented.

A reusable structure for describing a project without overstating its outcomes.

01 / Problem

What problem does this project address? What motivated the design?

02 / Design and architecture

Add diagrams, components, algorithms, constraints and design choices.

03 / Implementation

Document the software, hardware, tools, iterations and trade-offs.

04 / Results and evidence

Include measured results, photographs, video and source links once verified.

05 / Reflections

What worked, what failed and what would you improve?

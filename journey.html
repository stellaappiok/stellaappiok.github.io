Joining Ashesi as a Mastercard Foundation Scholar gave me an opportunity to deepen my foundations in engineering, computing, mathematics and design.

As an executive with Google Developer Groups at Ashesi, I have been growing through community, collaboration and technical exploration.

My practical work includes Python programs, robotic-arm control experiments, line-following control, embedded systems and CAD design. Each project is a chance to learn from real constraints.

I am reading and exploring research questions involving machine learning, autonomy and language models for robotic reasoning. This is an evolving direction, not a claim of published research.

I am developing a foundation in ROS 2 and simulation, connecting software engineering with autonomous robotic systems.

My next steps are to document stronger technical evidence, contribute to open-source projects and pursue opportunities to build intelligent systems with others.
